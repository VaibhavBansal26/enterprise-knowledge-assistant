Information Security Policy.

Passwords and access: passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is required for every account that can reach company data.

Incidents: report a suspected security incident to the security team within 24 hours. Do not attempt to investigate on your own or delete any evidence.

Devices: company laptops must use full-disk encryption and install operating system updates within 14 days of release.
