Northwind Labs Employee Handbook.

Remote work: employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10am to 3pm in the employee's local time zone.

Paid time off: full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per year. Unused days up to five may be carried over into the next year.

Equipment: each employee receives a laptop refresh budget of 1,500 dollars every three years. Monitors and keyboards are reimbursed up to 300 dollars.

Expenses: meals while travelling are reimbursed up to 50 dollars per day with receipts. Submit expense reports within 30 days of the purchase.
